\noindent The four texts below anchor the course: an applied spine
(Boyd \& Vandenberghe), a numerical-methods reference (Trefethen \& Bau), a
geometry/intuition source (Strang), and a theoretical backbone (Axler).
Bibliographic details should be verified against the exact printing adopted.
